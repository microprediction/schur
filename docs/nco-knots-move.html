Only the knots move

Under the gateway model the conditioning term of every cluster is supported on a single direction, so as γ runs along the bridge the member part of each cluster's direction does not move at all. Only the knot exposure changes. The page draws a random gateway covariance, four clusters with two to four assets each, and plots every entry of every cluster direction against γ.

7
What to look for: the solid lines, the knot exposures κi(γ), move. The dotted lines, one per remaining member, are flat to machine precision. At γ = 1 the stacked directions are the blocks of Σ−1u, and the portfolio is the global optimum; the readout reports the largest deviation.
What to look for: the excess variance of the bridge over the optimum, computed directly, against the closed form Lγ / (Z(Z − Lγ)) of the lost-precision proposition, on the same random model compressed to its knots. The two curves coincide, and vanish only at γ = 1.

Everything is computed live from the sampled covariance. The model has one knot per cluster; each remaining member loads on its own knot with a random beta and carries independent residual noise.
