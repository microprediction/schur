Example 2 — one-entry noise: interior without symmetry

The solved family perturbs all four cross entries in common. This example perturbs a single covariance entry, (1,3), by ±τ, so the common-noise symmetry is gone, and the interior optimum survives. The paper's certificate point is τ = 0.15 with γ̂ = 9/10: F(9/10) < F(0) and F(9/10) < F(1) as exact rational inequalities. Here the whole curve is drawn live at any τ.

What to look for: at τ = 0.15 the minimizer sits near γ = 0.9, matching the certificate. F(0) never moves as τ changes (Lemma 1 again: the noised entry is in the cross-block), while the right end rises with the noise.
