Demonstrations: the NCO bridge

Three live pages for A Schur Bridge from Nested Clustered Optimization to Global Minimum Variance.

Each page computes one result of the paper in your browser from the same formulas the paper states, with sliders where a parameter can be varied. The exact rational checks of the same claims are in paper/verify_schur_nco_bridge.py.

  • Identical clusters — the bridge in one number
    With identical clusters the portfolio is one scalar and the out-of-sample variance is a closed-form function of γ. The two-point estimate has a unique interior optimum at (1 + 2(k−2)c)/(2[1 + (k−3)c]), which is 2/3 in the simplest case, and small symmetric noise shows both signs of the endpoint shift with a threshold at δ = 8.
  • Effective damping — and the singular limit
    A common γ damps each cluster by λ = γr/(1 − γ + γr). As a knot becomes redundant the movement compresses into a boundary layer at γ = 1, the limits stop commuting, and the path in effective exposure runs continuously from 3/8 to 1/3.
  • Only the knots move
    On a random gateway covariance the member parts of every cluster direction are flat in γ to machine precision, only the knot exposures move, and the lost-precision identity holds along the whole bridge.
