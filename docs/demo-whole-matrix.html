Lemma 4 and Theorem 5 — whole-matrix noise: the sign of Ξ decides

The other demos noise only the cross-block. Here every entry of the covariance is perturbed: rank-one exchangeable displacements of amplitude eα on the A block, eδ on the D block, and eζ on the cross-block, with independent signs (8 equally likely states, averaged exactly). Theorem 5 says interiority is decided by the sign of Ξ = (9963/320)eζ² + (1674/125)eα² − (189/500)eδ², with 1 − γ* ≈ Ξ/((d−a)²c²K) at small amplitudes. Noise confined to the D block makes Ξ < 0 and full coupling stays optimal: the support of the noise does not decide the question, the sign of Ξ does.
