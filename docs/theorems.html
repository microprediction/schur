Check the theorems

Every result in The Minimum Out-of-Sample Variance Portfolio Lies Strictly Between Hierarchical Risk Parity and Minimum Variance, checked two ways.

Each theorem has a live demonstration, a page where your browser runs the paper's recursion at the paper's covariances, and an exact certificate, a rational-arithmetic verification in paper/neither_end_certificate.py. The demonstrations are one per page and live under Demos.

  • Lemma 1 — the HRP end is blind to the cross-block.
    At γ = 0 the recursion reads only the diagonal blocks, so F(0;τ) is exactly noise-free under cross-block noise. demo →
  • Theorem 1 — uniform separation from HRP under small noise.
    Whenever coupling has first-order population value, every minimizer stays away from γ = 0 for all sufficiently small noise, with no restriction on the noise support. demo →
  • Theorem 2 — the noise cost is γτ².
    F − V₀ = γτ²H with H bounded, and the exponent on γ cannot be improved. demo →
  • Example 1 — HRP becomes locally optimal at finite noise.
    Boundary derivative −1/700 + (8/189)τ², sign flip at τ* = √(27/800). demo →
  • Lemma 2 and Theorem 3 — small noise moves the optimum off the minimum-variance end.
    V₀′(1) = 0 automatically, and positive marginal noise sensitivity pushes the optimizer inside by order τ². demo →
  • Lemma 3, Proposition 2, and Theorem 4 — the solved family.
    Exact objective in closed form, strictly convex, unique interior minimizer at every admissible noise level, monotone from 1 to ½. demo →
  • Lemma 4 and Theorem 5 — whole-matrix noise.
    Every entry noisy; interiority decided by the sign of an explicit second-moment functional Ξ, and the condition is sharp. demo → entrywise → sample covariance →
  • Theorem 6 — the clipped frontier for the surrogate.
    The surrogate optimizer is the clipped inverse of t = −V₀′/G′; each endpoint owns an interval of noise levels unless its limit condition holds. demo →
  • Example 2 — one-entry noise, interior without symmetry.
    A single perturbed covariance entry, and the interior optimum survives: F(9/10) < min(F(0), F(1)) exactly at τ = 0.15. demo →
