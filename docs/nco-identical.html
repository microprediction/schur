Identical clusters — the bridge in one number

Give each of k clusters a knot of unit variance and one independent asset of variance 1/δ, with correlation c between every pair of knots. By symmetry a portfolio is one number, the total weight x on the knots, and the bridge maps an estimated correlation z to x through the closed-form knot exposure tγ(z). The expected out-of-sample variance is then a scalar function of γ, computed below from the formulas of the paper's Section 7.1 with no matrix inversion.

10 0.25
12.00 0.050
What to look for: with the two-point estimate, the minimum of the curve lands on the dashed line at the closed form γ⋆ = (1 + 2(k−2)c) / (2[1 + (k−3)c]) for every δ. At the default k = 10, c = ¼ that is 10/11, and at k = 2, c = ¼, δ = 1 it is exactly 2/3. With small symmetric noise the minimum sits at 1 − (G′(1)/V₀″(1))τ² when that is below 1, and at full coupling otherwise. At the default δ = 12 the same model stays at full coupling under small noise and moves to 10/11 under the two-point estimate. A curve whose minimum missed either line would falsify the paper.
What to look for: the sign of the small-noise shift coefficient as δ varies at the current k and c. For k = 10 and c = ¼ it is (8 − δ)/(1 + 13δ/4): positive below δ = 8, where the optimum moves inside, and negative above, where full coupling stays optimal.

The curves are the scalar formulas of the paper evaluated live. The same identities are checked in exact rational arithmetic by the verification script.
