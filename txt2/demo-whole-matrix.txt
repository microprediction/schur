 Lemma 4 and Theorem 5: under whole-matrix noise the sign of &Xi; decides

 The other demos noise only the cross-block. Here every entry of the
 covariance is perturbed: rank-one exchangeable displacements of amplitude
 e&alpha; on the A block, e&delta; on the D block, and
 e&zeta; on the cross-block, with independent signs (8 equally likely
 states, averaged exactly). Theorem 5 says interiority is decided by the sign of
 &Xi; = (9963/320)e&zeta;&sup2; + (1674/125)e&alpha;&sup2;
 &minus; (189/500)e&delta;&sup2;, with
 1 &minus; &gamma;* &asymp; &Xi;/((d&minus;a)&sup2;c&sup2;K) at small
 amplitudes. Noise confined to the D block makes &Xi; &lt; 0 and full coupling
 stays optimal: the support of the noise does not decide the question, the sign
 of &Xi; does.

 What to look for: with any noise on the cross or A
 channels the marked minimizer is interior and the measured 1 &minus; &gamma;*
