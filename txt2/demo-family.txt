 Theorem 4: the solved family has a unique interior optimum, monotone in the noise

 For Family A with common cross-block noise the paper computes the exact
 expected out-of-sample variance in closed form, equation (20). The solid curve
 is the recursion and the dashed curve is the closed form; they coincide. The
 objective is strictly convex, the minimizer is unique and interior for every
 0 &lt; e &le; c = 0.6, and it slides monotonically from &gamma;*(0) = 1 to
 &gamma;*(0.6) = &frac12; exactly.

 For e &ge; 0.15 the raw recursion is undefined at the single point
 &gamma; = 0.75/(0.6 + e), where a block's augmented fitness diverges. The
 recursion is evaluated on a grid that avoids that point, and the dashed closed
 form is the continuous extension (equation (18) of the paper), which the
 recursion equals everywhere else.
 Theorem 4(v) proves the minimizer always sits strictly left of that point, so
 the optimum is a statement about the recursion itself.
