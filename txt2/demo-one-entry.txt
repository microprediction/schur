 Example 2: one-entry noise, interior without symmetry

 The solved family perturbs all four cross entries in common. This example
 perturbs a single covariance entry, (1,3), by &plusmn;2&tau; (a correlation shift of &plusmn;&tau;), so the common-noise
 symmetry is gone, and the interior optimum survives. The paper's certificate
 point is &tau; = 0.15 with &gamma;&#770; = 9/10: F(9/10) &lt; F(0) and
 F(9/10) &lt; F(1) as exact rational inequalities. Here the whole curve is drawn
 live at any &tau;.

 What to look for: at &tau; = 0.15 the minimizer sits near
 &gamma; = 0.9, matching the certificate. F(0) never moves as &tau; changes
 (Lemma 1 again: the noised entry is in the cross-block), while the right end
 rises with the noise. A minimizer at 0 or 1 at &tau; = 0.15 would contradict the
 certificate.
