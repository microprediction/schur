 Lemma 1: HRP never sees the cross-block

 At &gamma; = 0 the recursion is hierarchical risk parity: it splits by the
 naive variances of the two diagonal blocks and never touches the cross-block. So
 noise in the cross-block cannot move the HRP weights at all, while any
 &gamma; &gt; 0 reads the noisy block and moves. The covariance is the paper's
 Family A: volatilities (1,1,2,2), within-block correlation &frac12;, cross-block
 correlation 0.3, and the noise moves the cross-block correlation by
 &plusmn;&tau;.

 cross-block noise &tau;

 0.10

 What to look for: drag &tau;. The &gamma; = 0 bars never
 move (clean and perturbed weights are identical to machine precision); the
 &gamma; = 0.6 bars drift with the noise. Any movement in the &gamma; = 0 bars would refute the lemma.
